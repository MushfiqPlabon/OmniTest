# SAT Writing: Essential Grammar and Transition Words

Transitions establish logical connections between ideas across paragraphs.
Additive transitions (furthermore, moreover, in addition) build on an established claim.
Contrastive transitions (however, nevertheless, on the other hand, conversely) signal a counterpoint or qualification.
Causal transitions (therefore, consequently, as a result, thus) identify effects proceeding from causes.
Run-on sentences occur when two independent clauses are joined without adequate punctuation or a coordinating conjunction (FANBOYS: for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so).
Comma splices occur when only a comma joins two complete thoughts; fix by inserting a semicolon, a period, or a comma plus coordinating conjunction.
Modifiers must be positioned directly adjacent to the noun they describe to prevent dangling modifiers.
