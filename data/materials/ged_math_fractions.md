# GED Math: Fractions, Decimals, and Percentages

Fractions represent parts of a whole where the top number is the numerator and the bottom number is the non-zero denominator.
To add or subtract fractions, you must find a common denominator (the least common multiple of both denominators).
To multiply fractions, multiply numerators straight across and multiply denominators straight across: (a/b) * (c/d) = (ac)/(bd).
To divide fractions, multiply by the reciprocal of the second fraction (keep, change, flip): (a/b) / (c/d) = (a/b) * (d/c).
To convert a fraction to a decimal, divide the numerator by the denominator.
To convert a decimal to a percentage, multiply by 100 and add the percent symbol (e.g., 0.75 becomes 75%).
On the GED exam, calculator-prohibited portions frequently test mental estimation of fractional equivalents like 1/8 = 0.125 = 12.5% and 3/4 = 0.75 = 75%.
